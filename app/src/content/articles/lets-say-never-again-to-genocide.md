This reflection by Dr. Joseph Ryarasa Nkurunziza was originally published on LinkedIn in April 2018, marking the commemoration of the Genocide against the Tutsi. It calls for "never again" to be understood as a continuous, lived responsibility rather than a repeated slogan — one that must be backed by education, strong institutions, and a shared commitment to protect human dignity.

Published in December 2025 under the title "The High Cost of Patronage", this piece reflects on Rwanda's local government from 1998 to 2008 and beyond. Dr. Nkurunziza warns that as administrative tools modernise, the quality of grassroots governance must not be allowed to quietly decline — and that patronage carries a cost that tools alone cannot fix.
